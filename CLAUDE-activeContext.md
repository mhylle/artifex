- **Four agentic patterns adopted (2026-07-26, [ADR-0006](docs/decisions/ADR-0006-agentic-patterns.md)):** reflection · tool use · planning · multi-agent collaboration, recorded as **principles in Tasktracker** (they now surface on every `setActiveTask`).
