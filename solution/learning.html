Chapter 4 · Memory & Learning

The trail is the treasure

Artifex's audit trail is not compliance paperwork — it is the raw material the system is refined from. Three stores with deliberately different rules keep evidence, earned capability, and earned knowledge separate; two learning loops with deliberately different speeds turn that material into improvement — and the very same event stream renders live to humans in mission control.

Three stores, three rulebooks

The separation is borrowed directly from what makes autoresearch work: evidence (every attempt, including failures), adopted capability (only measured wins), and raw output live in different places with different retention, different consumers, and different write rules. Mixing them is how systems end up either learning from noise or losing their history.

Audit Ledger

Rule: everything, forever, append-only.

  • Every decision, contract, exchange, attempt, verdict, budget movement and learning act — successes and failures alike, keyed by contract id and actor.
  • Failures are first-class: the sequence of what didn't work is exactly what the Learning Agent plans its next hypotheses from.
  • No agent can edit the past — including the Learning Agent. Tamper-evidence is constitutional.
  • Structured for querying, not archaeology: events carry typed error classes and criterion ids, so "which categories fail which clauses" is a lookup, not an investigation.

Asset Registry

Rule: only measured wins enter; every entry is versioned.

  • Agent designs, decomposition templates, category taxonomies, meta-agent playbooks, effort heuristics — the swarm's earned capability.
  • A ratchet: assets advance one validated delta at a time; equal-or-worse reverts automatically; simpler-is-better breaks ties.
  • Every version keyed to the ledger evidence that justified it — capability with a paper trail.
  • Retirement is down-weighting, never deletion; cold assets remain searchable as stepping stones.

Knowledge Commons

Rule: knowledge is earned, sourced, and mortal.

  • Validated findings about the world — facts, references, domain maps — reusable across missions.
  • Every entry carries provenance: which agent, which mission, which evidence, which verification.
  • New findings sit in quarantine until validated; high-impact findings require independent re-derivation before promotion.
  • Entries expire: knowledge has a shelf life and must periodically re-earn its place — stale certainty is worse than honest absence.

Two speeds of learning

Replay benchmarks — the trail as test bench

Every completed task in the ledger — its contract, its inputs, its verified outcome — is a potential benchmark. The Learning Agent evaluates candidate improvements by replaying them against work the swarm has already done, where ground truth is known. This gives the science loop what autoresearch had in its five-minute training runs: a fixed, comparable, affordable yardstick for every experiment.

  • The open bench is drawn from the general ledger and used freely for experimentation.
  • The sealed bench is a reserved slice of verified missions the Learning Agent never sees and never trains against — the constitutional instrument used to evaluate amendment petitions and to calibrate the Reviewer. Nothing that optimizes against a benchmark may also own it.
  • Benchmarks age out as the mission mix drifts — the bench is curated like the commons, not accumulated like the ledger.
